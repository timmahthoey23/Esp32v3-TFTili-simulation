StratoGlide Desk Display

A browser copy of the order screen, running the same boot sequence, dashboard, clock, celebrations and night mode as the sketch on your ESP32.

Screen on

Orders

Example numbers only. On the real screen new orders show within 30 seconds; here the sync is instant.

Device

BOOT plays a test celebration. At night it wakes the screen for 2 minutes.

Night mode

Sun–Thu off at 9 pm, Fri–Sat off at midnight, back on at 6 am every day (Perth time).

Close to the real thing, not identical. Fonts here are web fonts drawn to look like the Adafruit ones, and colours on the cheap TFT panel will be a little less vivid.

For GitHub Pages: save this as index.html in a repo and turn on Pages. Keep your API key out of it, since GitHub Pages sites are public.
